About the Project

DENKO OSINT is a platform for practical training in open-source intelligence (OSINT).

Mission

Popularization of OSINT among the Ukrainian community, training real skills, and creating a safe environment for practice.

Who is this project for?

For journalists, investigators, cybersecurity professionals, students, and anyone interested in OSINT.
